Five tiers, one DNA. Every button shares the same font, hover-lift, focus ring, and radius family. Color tells you the role; size tells you the importance. Pink + green are never used here — they're reserved for audience identity (Seniors / Grades 7–11).
Tier 1 · Primary
One per panel. The hero verb the user came to do. 14×22 · uppercase · radius 14
Tier 2 · Secondary
Everyday actions that sit beside Primary. 10×16 · sentence case · radius 12
Tier 3 · Destructive
Anything that wipes work or signs out. Same shape as Secondary, red palette.
Tier 4 · Ghost
Cancel, Skip, low-pressure escapes. Used inside modals next to a Primary.
Tier 5 · Icon
Square 36×36, used in the topbar and modal headers only.
