Class room connection

Use the HTTPS address your teacher provides. This page needs a running room service; the address for the solo game alone does not provide multiplayer.

Students

You receive a player number, then wait for your teacher to admit you. No name, email or chat is needed. Your teacher can pause or close the room.

Teachers

Use the password configured for your room service. It is not your school or Google password. Teacher access is kept only in this open page.

Your class rooms

Create a room, open it, and share its code. New players wait until you choose Admit / resume. Close pauses all student building and joining. Reopen lets the same group continue. Student controls are enforced by the room service.

Rooms and review evidence currently stay in the running room service's memory. Restarting the service clears them. This is not long-term project storage.

There is no chat. Teachers should still supervise builds. These controls do not automatically detect every word or image made from blocks. The activity log below contains service records; it is not a screenshot or an email alert.
