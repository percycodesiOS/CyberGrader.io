💾 Save or load a world

Keep your world

Save keeps your progress in this browser on this device. The game also saves as you play. Download Save puts a copy of your world in a file you can keep. It does not remove or change your world. Check your Downloads folder for the file ending in .json.

Load World opens a MYnecraft save file you choose. It asks before replacing the world saved in this browser. The two worlds do not join together. Keep both? Use Download Save first and check that your current world's file downloaded. Then choose Load World. Press Cancel at the warning to keep playing your current world.

To play on another device, move your save file there and open it with Load World in MYnecraft. This file cannot open in Minecraft; it only works in MYnecraft. There is no account or automatic device sync. The school and primary links share one save in the same browser. Clearing browser data can erase it. Reset World starts fresh after asking, so keep a downloaded copy first.

Autosave is local to this browser. Keep a downloaded backup of builds you want to keep.

More tools & building guides
🧭 Travel to coordinates

Creative only. X and Z move across the map; Y is eye height. Go starts flight at those coordinates if the spot is clear. If it is inside a block, try a higher Y. The crosshair aims at blocks. Settings shows your current position.

Your world has edges: X −95 to 127, Z −95 to 95.

🪧 Name your spaces

Walk to a space, open Menu, then add a short area name. Small signs stay at that spot and show only when you are nearby. Name the space, such as Music Studio or Quiet Corner.

Up to 40 signs. They save with your world and downloaded backup.

💧 Build a water elevator
  1. Place one Glowstone block on the ground.
  2. Build a shaft with Glass around it. Leave a doorway at the bottom and an exit at the top.
  3. Place Water in every space directly above the Glowstone. A gap stops the lift.
  4. Turn flight off and walk into the water. It lifts you! Walk out at the top. Hold V or DOWN to ride down.

This MYnecraft recipe uses Glowstone. Ordinary water still works for swimming. The lift works in both game modes; in Embrace the Challenge, collect the blocks first.

⌨️ All the controls

Choose your blocks: Open Blocks / Inventory or press I while playing. Choose a pictured material to keep building. Creative has unlimited blocks; Embrace the Challenge shows what you have collected. Back to game, I, or Esc closes the picker. The block bar still works too.

Keyboard and mouse: W Forward · S Back · A Left · D Right. The arrow keys also move: ↑ Forward · ↓ Back · ← Left · → Right. Move your mouse to look, or click and drag if it stays free. Space jumps or swims up; Shift sprints on foot. Left click breaks a block, and right click places the selected block. Use 1–9, [ and ], the scroll wheel, or the block bar to change materials.

Creative flight and camera: Press F once to turn flight on or off. While flying, Space goes up and V goes down; let go to hover. Hold Shift to move faster on foot or in flight without changing height. C switches between first and third person; Menu → Settings has the same choice.

Phone or tablet: Choose Touch to show the screen buttons, or Auto to follow your device. Move with the stick and drag the screen to look. Tap the block bar, BREAK, or PLACE. Hold JUMP to swim or fly upward. In Creative, FLY toggles flight. DOWN descends while flying or in a water elevator. Phones and tablets never capture a mouse.

Pause and keep playing: Esc releases the mouse and opens this menu. The Menu button also pauses. Use Play to continue, Save to keep local progress now, and Download Save for a copy you can keep or move.
