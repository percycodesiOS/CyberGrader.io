# Voice Examples — Mr. Macek's voice, polished register

Reference rewrites of variation templates in the new register: full sentences, real punctuation, no slang. Use these as the bar for any future generated copy.

## 25% Progress Check-In — All Students

Subject: Cyber Progress Check-In — 25% Mark

Hi Students,

Quick check-in. We are at the 25% mark of the term, and your current grade reflects what you have turned in so far. If you have missing or partial work, this is a great point to catch up — there is plenty of time, but the gap gets harder to close the longer it sits.

If anything is unclear or you are stuck, reply to this email and we will figure it out together.

— Mr. Macek

## Voice Rules Summary

- Full sentences with subjects and verbs.
- Em-dashes and commas, never ellipses-as-style.
- Warmth comes from word choice, not slang or shortened forms.
- Closer is consistent: "reply to this email" or "let me know" — followed by a sign-off on its own line.
- Sign-off is **always** `— Mr. Macek` (em-dash + space + name).
- Audience-aware: "Hi Students," for groups, "Hi (student name)," for singles, "Hi Seniors," for senior-specific.
