## House style — quick reference

- Em dashes ( — ) for asides, never two hyphens.
- Periods, not comma splices.
- Sign off **"I am here if you have any questions"** or **"Reach out anytime."** Pick one per email.
- "I am" / "you are" / "we will" — no contractions in formal student-facing emails. Contractions are fine in feedback that goes into the gradebook (it is shorter and reads more personal there).
- No exclamation points unless genuinely celebrating perfect work, and never more than one per message.
- Bold the deadline date in any email that has one.
