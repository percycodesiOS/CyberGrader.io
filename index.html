A world to explore

Make it yours

Build beside the lake or head beyond the campus to find your next project. Choose your blocks, start small, and see where your ideas take you. Creative Inventory also has trees, flowers, and a complete fishing pond kit.

Visit EC

Walk through the glass entrance and find BELIEVE. Follow the drop-off circle to the playground, basketball court, and outdoor classroom. Every basket sends confetti above the hoop.

Meet the crew

Meet Sleeves in his number 28 jersey on the front lawn. Officer Shields and Mr. Eiler stay near the entrance; Mr. B carries his laptop through the halls. Ms. Micco patrols campus, KaY follows Sir D, and Ellie and Percy run together and return to your character. Sir D and Mr. Macek meet near the entrance around sunset.

Explore the school: Find the flag in the entrance circle, landscaped parking rows, and two glass doorways into the atrium. The main cafeteria has a high ECTV screen; the elementary cafeteria is on the left. Grade 5 is on the right with Grade 6 above; Grades 3–4 are on the left with Grades 1–2 above. Follow the broad curved walkway to Kindergarten. The school is a playable, scaled interpretation of the real campus.

The campus runs on game time: The School Officer parks beyond the far curb near the circle exit at 8:30 AM, patrols the cafeteria, and starts walking back to his car at 3:00 PM. He is separate from Officer Shields. The cafeteria custodian drives the floor scrubber from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Three buses start their rear-loop trip at 3:00 PM. Cookie Man sweeps from 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Eddie is on the lawn rock by day, starts flying to the roof at 6:00 PM, and returns by 6:00 AM. Auto sleep skips night after a one-second rest at 6:00 PM. Choose Full day/night for the original 20-minute cycle, or Always day for a bright sky while the clock runs. The menu pauses time.

How to play

Keyboard & mouse

W Forward · S Back · A Left · D Right.
Arrow keys work too: ↑ Forward · ↓ Back · ← Left · → Right.
Move your mouse, or click and drag, to look.
Space to jump or swim up. Shift moves faster on foot or in flight. F toggles flight in Creative.
While flying: Space up, V down. Let go to hover.
Left click to break. Right click to place.
Use 1–9, [ and ], scroll, or the block bar to choose a material.
C changes the camera view. Esc releases the mouse and pauses.

iPad & touch

Use the joystick to move. Drag the screen to look.
Tap the block bar to choose your material.
Use the on-screen buttons to jump, place, or break.
In Creative, FLY toggles flight, JUMP rises, and DOWN descends.
Open Menu whenever you want to pause.
Embrace the Challenge is peaceful; falls and deep water can hurt.

Choose your controls: Auto follows this device. Choose Touch for screen buttons or Keyboard and mouse for computer controls. You can change this in the menu or toolbar.

Your own fishing pond: In Creative, open Inventory and choose Fishing pond under Trees & garden kits. Aim at clear, flat ground and PLACE. Or build a 2 by 2 tank with a solid bottom, sides, and four water blocks. Add Fish from Inventory. They grow in water over two minutes of active play. Choose Fishing rod, face a grown fish, then press E or tap Interact. Keep close to the pond until you catch it. Fish growth and your catch total save with each world.

Less on screen: Press H to hide or show the movement help. On touch screens, use the Show movement help setting in the menu.

Name your spaces: Walk to a space, then use Menu > Name your spaces to add, edit, or remove a small area sign. Signs stay where you place them and save with your world.

Build a water elevator: Place Glowstone at the bottom, then Water directly above it with no gaps. Build Glass walls and leave a bottom doorway and top exit. Turn flight off and walk in to rise; hold V or DOWN to descend. Find the full recipe in the game menu.

Find your way: In the campus world, open the menu to visit the ECMS entrance, change outfits or camera views, or fly above the school and travel to coordinates in Creative. The visit shortcuts keep the current game time. Choose Smooth graphics for faster play. Creative and regular Embrace the Challenge share your campus build. Hardcore opens a separate resource world.

Keep your world

Save keeps your progress in this browser on this device. The game also saves as you play. Download Save puts a copy of your world in a file you can keep. Your world stays here. Look in Downloads for the file ending in .json.

Load World opens a MYnecraft save file you choose. It asks before replacing this browser's current world. The two worlds do not join together. To keep both, use Download Save first and check that the file downloaded. Press Cancel at the warning if you are not ready.

You can move that file to another device and open it in MYnecraft. It cannot open in Minecraft. Solo worlds do not automatically sync across devices. The school and primary links share one save in the same browser, containing your campus and separate Hardcore world. Clearing browser data can erase both. Keep a downloaded copy before resetting.

Teacher rooms require a separately configured room service and teacher admission. They keep group building separate from your solo saves. No chat or text signs are available. The current room service needs teacher supervision; automatic detection of words built from blocks and screenshot alerts are not yet configured.
