Things to do

In the game, press Menu and pick one from Things to try.

  • 🧱

    Build anything

  • 🚗

    Drive the car

    Find the blue car in the parking lot, walk up and press Drive car.

  • 🏀

❓ How to play

⌨️ Keyboard & mouse

  • W A S D or arrow keys to walk
  • Move the mouse to look; click and drag if it stays still
  • Right click places a block, left click removes one
  • E to drive, shoot, sit, read or call the dogs
  • I opens Inventory · Space jumps · Shift is faster
  • Creative: F to fly, Space up, V down
  • C changes the camera · Esc opens the menu

📱 Tablet, phone or touch

  • Move with the round stick; drag the screen to look
  • Tap PLACE to add a block and BREAK to remove one
  • Tap the yellow button to drive, shoot, sit or read
  • Creative: FLY turns flight on; hold JUMP to rise, DOWN to drop
  • 🙈 Hide buttons clears the screen; tap it again to bring them back
  • On a laptop in Touch mode, the buttons work with the mouse too

💾 Keep your world

Your build is saved in this browser on this device, every few seconds and when you leave. For a copy you can keep, open Menu → Save or load a world → Download Save and keep the .json file somewhere safe, like your school OneDrive. Load World opens that file on another device and asks before replacing anything. Clearing browser data erases the saved world, so keep a copy.

👩‍🏫 For teachers

Teacher rooms need a separately set-up room service and teacher admission, and keep group building apart from solo saves. There is no chat or text signs. The room service needs teacher supervision; automatic detection of words built from blocks is not set up yet. Open teacher rooms.
