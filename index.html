How to play

Keyboard & mouse

W A S D to move. Mouse or click and drag to look.
Space to jump. F toggles flight in Creative.
While flying: Space up, Shift down.
Left click to break. Right click to place.
Scroll or select the block bar to choose a material.

iPad & touch

Use the joystick to move. Drag the screen to look.
Tap the block bar to choose your material.
Use the on-screen buttons to jump, place, or break.
Open Menu whenever you want to pause.
Embrace the Challenge is peaceful; falls and deep water can hurt.

Find your way: Open the game menu to visit ECMS, change outfits, or travel to coordinates in Creative. Choose Smooth graphics for faster play. You can switch modes without clearing your build.

Keep your world

Autosave keeps this world in this browser on this device only. It does not sync to another device, an account, or a multiplayer world. Download World saves a portable backup JSON of your blocks, position, settings, and outfit. Load World imports a backup you choose and, after you confirm, replaces the world on this device. It does not merge two worlds. Download your current world first if you want to keep it. Clearing browser data can erase the local save.
