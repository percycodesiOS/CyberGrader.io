Keyboard & mouse
W A S D to move. Mouse to look.

Left click to break. Right click to place.
Scroll or tap the block bar to choose a block.

iPad & touch
Use the joystick to move and drag the screen to look.
Tap the block bar to choose your material.
Use the on-screen buttons to jump, place, or break.
Open Menu to visit the school or change modes.
