Things to do

Open the menu in the game and tap Things to try to jump straight to any of these.

  • 🧱

    Build anything

    Choose a block in Inventory, aim the + and place it. Trees, gardens and ponds go down in one tap.

  • 🚗

    Drive the car

    Find the blue car in the front parking lot. Walk up and press Drive car. It rolls right over curbs.

  • 🏀

    Shoot hoops

    Pick up the ball on the court and shoot. Closer shots go in more often. A basket means a big confetti burst!

  • ☕

    Tea on the roof

    Choose Cup of tea, find a cozy spot up high, and sit down to enjoy the view.

  • 📚

    Your own library

    Build with Bookshelf blocks or place the Little library kit. Face a shelf and a story is read out loud.

  • 🐕

    Percy & Ellie

    The dogs play on their own near home. Walk up to them to ask them to follow you, or let them play.

  • 🎣

    Go fishing

    Build a pond, add fish, wait for them to grow, then catch one with the fishing rod.

  • 🏫

    Explore EC

    Walk through the glass doors to find BELIEVE, the cafeteria screen, the gym and your classroom wing.

How to play

Four moves are all you need. Everything else is in the menu.

⌨️ Keyboard & mouse

  1. WASDWalk around. Arrow keys work too.
  2. MOUSELook around. Click and drag if it stays still.
  3. CLICKRight click places a block. Left click removes one.
  4. EDo things: drive, shoot, sit, read, call the dogs.

F flies in Creative · Space up · V down · Esc opens the menu

📱 Tablet, phone or touch

  1. STICKMove with the round stick at the bottom left.
  2. DRAGDrag anywhere on the screen to look around.
  3. PLACETap PLACE to add a block, BREAK to remove it.
  4. YELLOWTap the yellow button to drive, shoot, sit or read.

Want to see the whole screen? Tap 🙈 Hide buttons. Tap it again to bring them back. On a laptop in Touch mode, the buttons work with the mouse too.

Keep your world

Your build stays on this device, in this browser.

💾

It saves by itself

The game saves as you build, every half minute and when you leave. The Save button saves right away.

📥

Download a copy

Menu → Save, download or load a world → Download Save. Keep the .json file somewhere safe, like your school OneDrive.

🔁

Another device?

Open your file with Load World. It asks before replacing anything. Clearing browser data erases the saved world, so keep a copy.

For teachers

Teacher rooms need a separately set-up room service and teacher admission, and keep group building apart from solo saves. There is no chat or text signs. The room service needs teacher supervision; automatic detection of words built from blocks is not set up yet.
